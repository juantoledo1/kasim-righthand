**What a Pareto EA does:**

- Email & calendar — inbox triage, scheduling, communication, follow-up
- Business operations — project management, process docs, vendor coordination, SOPs
- Research & analysis — market/competitor research, reporting, trend monitoring
- Personal & lifestyle — travel, expense tracking, errands, lifestyle management
- Content & marketing — social scheduling, content calendar, newsletter, campaigns

**Why Kasim recommends it:**

- Frees up 20+ hours per week
- Handles operations so you can focus on growth
- Consistent, reliable support with timezone flexibility
- Delivers 10x ROI through increased productivity

## Ivan Bunin — Co-Founder, Pareto Talent

> **Update:** this section replaces the earlier "Business partner at Pareto Talent — unnamed in sources" placeholder. The interview transcript only said *"I own Pareto Talent with a business partner"*; the identity is now confirmed by the official Pareto Talent website (`paretotalent.com/about`), which frames the partnership as "Co-Founded by a Founder and His Former EA."

**Origin story:**

- Kasim found Ivan on a freelancer site (Freelancer.com) when Ivan was 18.
- Ivan went from executive assistant to CTO, helped sell the company, and became Kasim's business partner.

**Kasim's side:**

- 15 years hiring EAs, with dozens of failures along the way.
- Built **Solutions 8** into the #1 Google Ads agency worldwide with nearly 100 employees — but kept hitting the same wall with assistants until Ivan.

**Ivan's side:**

- Started at 18 as an EA hired from a freelancer site, with no playbook.
- Went from managing an inbox to managing an entire company's technology.
- Managed the due diligence for an **8-figure acquisition** (the Solutions 8 exit, 2022).

**Trajectory (official journey, `paretotalent.com/about`):**

- **2018** — Hired by Kasim from Freelancer.com as his EA; Solutions 8 had 6 employees; Ivan's first job.
- **2018–2022** — Rose from EA to Tech Lead to CTO; Solutions 8 scaled to 200+ clients, #1 Google Ads agency worldwide.
- **2022** — 8-figure exit: Solutions 8 acquired; Ivan managed the entire financial and operational due diligence.
- **Today** — Ivan's EA **Ampy** was the very first internal hire at Pareto Talent (Kasim now has 3 EAs from Pareto).

**Mission:** place 1,000 founders with 1,000 Right Hands (100+ founders served so far, per the official site).

# Company History

A written history of Kasim's companies and track record. **Starter note:** the intake files document dates and numbers but not a year-by-year narrative; the official Pareto Talent website (`paretotalent.com`) now provides one for the Solutions 8 → Pareto Talent story. This file records exactly what each source supports, attributes every claim, and explicitly flags where sources differ. It will grow as the brain is fed.

## Documented timeline (from `textWeb.md`)

- **2010–present** — Self-taught entrepreneur, 15+ years. Focus: business building, exits, scaling, delegation, team management. The authority hub lists the track-record timeframe as 2010–present.
- **2019** — Founded **3X Freedom** (free entrepreneur community; entrepreneurship education, business coaching).
- **2020** — Co-founded **Pareto Talent** (executive assistant placement; business services) — per the authority hub (`textWeb.md`). **Sources differ:** the official journey on `paretotalent.com` dates the paretotalent.com domain to late 2023 and the first EA graduation to April 2024. Both facts are recorded here with attribution rather than merged into a single story.
- **2024** — Founded **AEO Community** (Answer Engine Optimization education) and **VCinc.ai** (AI development education).

## Official journey — Solutions 8 → Pareto Talent (from `paretotalent.com`)

The official "Journey" section on the Pareto Talent site documents the partnership timeline behind the company:

- **2018** — Kasim hires Ivan Bunin from Freelancer.com as his EA; Solutions 8 had 6 employees; it was Ivan's first job.
- **2018–2022** — Ivan rises from EA to Tech Lead to CTO; Solutions 8 scales to 200+ clients and becomes the **#1 Google Ads agency worldwide**.
- **2022** — **8-figure exit:** Solutions 8 is acquired; Ivan manages the entire financial and operational due diligence.
- **Late 2023** — A failed startup (after a stalled self-storage pivot) leads to "paretotalent.com" sitting in Kasim's domain list.
- **April 2024** — First EA graduates; first client **Tim Bratton**; a scheduled 30-minute call runs 2.5 hours; **$2,097** total startup cost.
- **May 2025** — **$1M in revenue**; celebrated in Lake Como, Italy; zero ads, 100% word-of-mouth; Kasim's **6th million-dollar business**, Ivan's first at 27.
- **Today** — 100+ founders served, building toward 1,000; **93% retention at 12 months**; every client from referral.

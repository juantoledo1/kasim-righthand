## 1) FOTO DEL FOUNDER (CASIM) — con slogan "GET 20 HOURS BACK"

### Opción A — EDITAR la foto de LinkedIn que bajaste (mantiene su cara)
Subí la foto a ChatGPT y pegá:

> "This is a LinkedIn profile photo of a male founder in his early 40s: round profile shot, yellow background, only head and shoulders visible, shirt barely visible. Extend and rebuild the image into a professional half-body portrait: keep his exact face, facial features and expression. Change the background from yellow to a clean, bright white studio background with soft lighting. Dress him in a premium navy blue t-shirt with a small, elegant white slogan on the chest reading 'GET 20 HOURS BACK'. Realistic photo quality, sharp, professional corporate photography, 4K."

### Opción B — Regenerar estilo Kasim (si la edición no extiende bien)
> "Professional corporate half-body portrait of a confident male founder in his early 40s, short dark hair, light stubble, wearing a premium navy blue t-shirt with small elegant white text 'GET 20 HOURS BACK' on the chest, warm genuine smile, bright clean white studio background, soft lighting, high-end business photography, sharp focus, 4K."

> Tips para no gastar intentos: si el texto sale mal, probá "a small white slogan on the chest" sin las palabras exactas y lo arreglás después. Si no extiende la foto, decile "outpaint / extend the image downward".
